Rules

Spreads only. American odds are converted to decimal; if the feed has no price, 1.91 (−110) is used and marked *.
Line and odds lock when you place the bet.
Parlay: any losing leg loses the parlay. A pushed or postponed leg is removed and the parlay pays on the remaining legs.
Round robin: every combination of the chosen size(s) is a separate parlay at the same stake. Total stake = stake × number of combos.
Data source: ESPN public feed.
